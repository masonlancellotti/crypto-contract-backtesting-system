# Three new paths: cross-coin, ladder-arb, settlement-TWAP (2026-06-15)

> The paths not chosen earlier, pursued diligently. All READ-ONLY; live disabled.
> Meta-frame: every prior failure was a *single-instrument predictive bet*; these test
> *relationships between instruments* and *structural/mechanical* edges instead.

## Path 1 — cross-coin relative value (BTC leads alts) — `kalshi-ade-mine-crosscoin`
Attach BTC's concurrent underlying state (implied prob, distance-z, 60/180s returns) at each
alt window's decision time via a fine-grained BTC clock; pool the 4 alts (rank-normalized);
gauntlet. 678 pooled windows (BTC matched 593), 496 search / 168 holdout, 280 trials.

- **Real cross-coin lead-lag — the strongest residual IC any mine produced:** BTC's implied prob
  predicts an alt's outcome (IC_out **+0.63**) *and* its market RESIDUAL (IC_resid **+0.28**,
  stab 0.80); btc_dist_z +0.27; btc_ret_180s +0.15. BTC genuinely leads the alts and the alt
  markets are slightly slow to price it.
- **But NO tradeable edge:** best rule DSR 0.024, **PBO 0.59 (overfit)**, plateau spike, mixed
  per-asset (ETH/XRP +, SOL −). Economic reason: alt spreads are **3–4× BTC's** (1.2–3.8c), which
  eats the thin lead-lag residual. **Real signal, no cost-clearing bridge — now confirmed at the
  cross-sectional level.** (The most interesting near-miss of the whole effort.)

## Path 2 — live strike-ladder arbitrage — `scripts/research/ladder_arb_scan.py`
For a ≥K ladder, {buy YES(≥K1)+NO(≥K2)} (K1<K2) always pays ≥$1, so ask_yes(K1)+ask_no(K2)<1 = riskless arb.

- **No arbitrage.** Directional ≥K ladders **KXBTCD** (188 strikes) and **KXSOLD** (75) are tight
  at exactly **1.0100** (= the 1c spread, no edge), monotonic, internally consistent. Range
  series (KXETH/KXBTC) bucket-asks sum to **2.0–3.1** (no buy-all arb).
- **Process note:** a first naive scan flagged false "arbs" on the range series by misapplying the
  ≥K basket to `less`/`between` markets; caught because the clean ladder read exactly 1.01. Fixed
  to be strike-type-aware. Byproduct: a reusable ladder-consistency scanner + confirmation that
  KXBTCD is clean & liquid for the eventual ⑥ vol-coherence study (still data-gated on recording).

## Path 3 — settlement-TWAP final-60s mechanic — `scripts/research/twap_settlement_probe.py`
Markets settle on a ~60s-average index, so in the final minute part of the settlement is frozen.
At 25s-to-close (n=430 BTC hires windows), reconstruct the partial average and test if "frozen
distance" predicts beyond instantaneous spot.

- **No edge.** frozen_dist and inst_dist have **identical** IC — outcome +0.865 vs +0.864,
  residual **+0.629 vs +0.629**. The averaging adds nothing: over the final 35s the spot barely
  moves vs the distance, so frozen ≈ instantaneous. The +0.629 residual IC is the *same* late-window
  distance/reprice-lag effect (#8/#23/#④), not a TWAP edge; after-cost trade n=3, −33c.

## Verdict across BTC and all coins
Three structurally-different attacks → **same wall.** Cross-coin lead-lag is *real* but the alts'
wide spreads kill it; the ladders are *arbitrage-free*; the TWAP mechanic is *informationally
redundant* with spot. No tradeable edge, with proof in each case. The cross-coin residual IC (+0.28)
is the single most promising signal surfaced — worth a forward re-test ONLY if alt spreads tighten
or an alt becomes more liquid (its untradeability is a microstructure/cost fact, not a calibration one).
